Car Care privacy policy

Last updated October 19, 2026

  • Car Care stores your cars, maintenance tasks, service history and odometer readings on your phone.
  • If you choose "Connect Google Sheets", the app asks for Google's drive.file permission. This lets it create and update one spreadsheet named "Car Care" in your own Google Drive. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
  • Your data goes only to your phone and to that spreadsheet in your Google account. It is not sent to the developer, sold, shared or used for advertising. There is no tracking or analytics.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions, and you can delete the spreadsheet yourself from Google Drive.
  • Contact: zcalbri@gmail.com
