Car Care

A personal Android app that tracks car maintenance and reminds you when something is due.

What it does

  • Keeps a list of maintenance jobs for each of your cars. You pick the jobs and how often each repeats, by miles, months, or both.
  • Sends a notification when a job is overdue or coming up.
  • Records service history: date, odometer, cost, notes, and whether you or a shop did the work.
  • Optionally backs up to a Google Sheet in your own Google Drive.

Google access

If you tap "Connect Google Sheets", the app asks for the drive.file permission. It can only create and update the one spreadsheet it makes. It cannot see any other file in your Drive.
